Your 12 words, nothing else

Built in: Wafer Shield

Pay privately. Nobody sees who paid whom, or how much.

On a public blockchain anyone can follow your payments and your balance. Wafer Shield gives you a private balance next to your public one, on BNB Chain and Ethereum, for BNB, ETH, USDT and USDC.

  1. ShieldMove funds from your wallet into your private balance.
  2. Send privatelyPay any shielded address. The amount, the sender and the receiver stay hidden.
  3. UnshieldTake funds out to any public address, your own included, whenever you like.

A zero-knowledge proof is made on your device, so no server ever learns what you do. Only you can spend your shielded funds; nobody can freeze or take them, Wafer included. Moving funds in costs 0.5%.

Wafer Shield also works on the web with any wallet

New: Wafer Keep

Turn an old phone into a hardware wallet.

Keep your keys on an old Android phone that never goes online. Wafer Wallet prepares the payment; Wafer Keep shows exactly what it does and signs it with your PIN. The two talk only through encrypted QR codes, so your keys never touch the internet.

  1. Seal the phoneOne command from your Mac installs Wafer Keep and locks the phone’s Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, mobile data and factory reset.
  2. ConnectTap Connect Wafer Keep in Wafer Wallet and scan one code each way. Both screens show the same 10 digits.
  3. Use it like any walletEthereum-style networks, Bitcoin and Solana, with swaps and dApps. Every send is signed on Wafer Keep: check it there, hold to sign, scan the answer back.

Quantum-safe pairing (ML-KEM-768 with P-256) and AES-256 on every code. Wafer Keep talks only to Wafer Wallet, and Wafer Wallet checks every signature before it sends anything. It never signs account delegations (EIP-7702) or transactions signed ahead, and can hold you to trusted addresses and daily limits. The Keep app has no internet permission at all. Free: no device to buy.

What you approve is what happens.

  • Plain review screens. Transfers, token approvals and signatures are decoded into amounts, addresses and the app asking.
  • Simulated first. Each transaction is tried on several independent nodes before you approve, so you see what you send and receive, and a transaction that would fail is caught.
  • Tapjacking blocked. A site or an app drawn over the wallet can't trick you into tapping Approve: the buttons that send or approve wait a moment every time they appear or the wallet comes back to the front.
  • No hidden frames, no screenshots. The wallet refuses to run inside another page (clickjacking). On Android, screenshots and screen recordings of the wallet are blocked; your recovery words are only ever shown in parts.
  • Risky approvals flagged. Unlimited token approvals, “approve all” NFT access and look-alike site names get a clear warning.
  • PIN or fingerprint for every send. Nothing leaves your wallet without your 6-digit PIN or your fingerprint. After 3 wrong PINs the wallet makes you wait, longer each time, so a stolen device can’t be guessed open. The PIN is also tied to your device’s secure hardware (the phone’s security chip, the Mac’s Keychain, Windows’ protected storage), so a copy of the wallet’s files can’t be guessed at on another computer.
  • Built for the quantum era. Everything stored on your device is sealed with AES-256 and SHA-256, and the link to Wafer Keep uses ML-KEM-768, a post-quantum standard. The networks’ own signatures are up to each network; for Bitcoin, change goes to a new address of your wallet, never back to one whose public key a spend has already exposed.
  • Offline keys, if you want. With Wafer Keep your keys live on an old phone that never goes online, and every signature is approved on its own screen.
  • Keys never leave the device. Wafer has no server that sees your keys, balances or history, and keeps no account for you.

Questions

How do I move my wallet to a new phone or computer?

Install the app there, choose Restore and type your 12 or 24 words, then choose a PIN. Your addresses and funds stay the same. Wallets made with an older version (passkey, password and recovery kit) move the same way: with their words.

Does Wafer hold my keys or my money?

No. The wallet runs on your device and Wafer never sees your keys. Your 12 words are the only backup: lose your device and the words, and nobody can recover the wallet for you.

What does it cost?

The app is free. You pay the network's own fees when you send. Swaps and bridges happen in the dApps you choose, such as LI.FI, which set their own fees; Wafer adds nothing. Wafer Shield takes 0.5% when you move funds into it.

Can anyone freeze my shielded funds?

No. Wafer Shield runs on contracts on BNB Chain and Ethereum that nobody, Wafer included, can pause or change: they can't freeze, move or delay what you shield, and you can always unshield to a public address. Check that private payments are allowed where you live.

What is Wafer Keep?

A free app that turns an old Android phone into a hardware wallet for Ethereum-style networks, Bitcoin and Solana. The phone stays offline and holds the keys; connected to it, Wafer Wallet on your everyday phone or computer works as a full wallet, and Wafer Keep signs every send after you check it on its screen. They talk only through encrypted QR codes. Keep your Keep’s recovery words on paper: they are its only backup.

Which networks does it support?

Ethereum, BNB Chain, Base, Arbitrum, Polygon, OP Mainnet, Avalanche and 20+ more Ethereum-style networks, plus Bitcoin and Solana (SOL, USDC and USDT). The same recovery words cover all of them.

Why no web wallet?

An app can check the wallet's code against BNB Chain before running it, keep its secret in your device's secure hardware and keep working without any website. A web page can't promise all three, so the wallet runs only in the app.

What if I forget my PIN?

A PIN can’t be reset or looked up, because it is the key that encrypts the wallet on your device. Remove the wallet from the device and restore it from its 12 or 24 words, then choose a new PIN.
