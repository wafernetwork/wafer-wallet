Before you start

Setting up takes about ten minutes. Any spare phone works: it never needs a SIM card or Wi-Fi again.

  • An old Android phoneAndroid 9 or newer, with a locked bootloader. Everything on it will be erased.
  • A USB cableTo connect the phone to your Mac, once.
  • A MacWith Terminal, to run the setup command.
  • Paper and a penFor your recovery words, the only backup of the keys.

Set up the phone

Do these in order. After the last step the phone can only run Wafer Keep.

  1. Reset the phone

    Factory reset it. During setup skip everything: no Wi-Fi, no Google account. Then take out the SIM card.

  2. Turn on USB debugging

    Settings → About phone → tap Build number seven times. Then Settings → Developer options → USB debugging on.

  3. Install Android's USB tool on your Mac

    Once, in Terminal: brew install android-platform-tools

  4. Connect and run the setup command

    Plug in the phone and tap Allow on it. Paste the command from the top of this page into Terminal. It downloads Wafer Keep, checks its fingerprint, installs it and seals the phone. Type SEAL when it asks.

  5. Create your keys

    Unplug the phone. In Wafer Keep tap Create new keys, write the 24 words on paper, and choose a PIN.

What sealing locks

Wafer Keep has no internet permission, so Android blocks every connection it might try. Sealing also locks the phone itself, so it can't be put back online by accident or by someone else.

  • Wi-Fi
  • Bluetooth
  • Mobile data and SIM
  • Airplane mode stays on
  • Factory reset and safe mode
  • USB file transfer
  • Installing other apps
  • USB debugging

What still works

The screen, the camera for reading QR codes, and the power button.

To use the phone for something else later, open Wafer Keep → Settings → Unseal this phone. It asks for your PIN.

Use it with Wafer Wallet

Wafer Wallet on your everyday phone or computer works as a full wallet for your Keep: Ethereum-style networks, Bitcoin and Solana. Wafer Keep is the only one that can sign.

Connect, once

  1. On Wafer Wallet's opening screen tap Connect Wafer Keep (or, in a wallet you already have: Menu → Wafer Keep).
  2. In Wafer Keep: Pair with Wafer Wallet, and hold the camera on the wallet's code.
  3. Scan Wafer Keep's code with the wallet the same way.
  4. Check both screens show the same 10 digits.

Use it like any wallet

  1. Wafer Wallet then shows your Keep accounts on every network, with balances, history, swaps and dApps. Send as usual.
  2. In Wafer Keep, tap Scan to sign, check the amount and address, and enter your PIN.
  3. Scan Wafer Keep's answer with the wallet. It checks the signature and sends.

Why you can trust it

Encrypted, and quantum-safe

Every code between the two apps is encrypted with AES-256. Pairing uses ML-KEM-768 together with P-256, so recordings of the codes stay unreadable even to a future quantum computer.

Only Wafer Wallet

Wafer Keep opens requests only from wallets it is paired with. Wafer Wallet accepts signatures only from its Keep, and checks each one before sending anything.

Guarded keys

The keys are encrypted with your PIN and sealed by the phone's secure hardware. Ten wrong PINs in a row erase them; your paper words bring them back.

Nothing signed ahead, nothing handed over

Wafer Keep never signs an account delegation (EIP-7702), the trick used to drain the Binance hot wallet in October 2026, nor a Solana transaction that never expires. It remembers the last nonce it signed on each network and stops you if a transaction is signed ahead, so it can't be held and sent later. And it refuses what it can't read (contract calls of most apps, new contracts, other programs' Solana instructions) unless you allow those in its settings.

Your own limits

Optional rules, kept on the offline phone and changed only with your PIN: sign only for trusted addresses, and send at most a set amount of each coin per day. A hacked phone or computer running Wafer Wallet can't switch them off.

Questions

Do I have to seal the phone?

No. You can install the app file on any phone with airplane mode on and no SIM card. Wafer Keep still has no internet permission. Sealing adds the locks above, which is safer for a phone kept as a wallet.

What if I forget the PIN?

After ten wrong tries the keys are erased from the phone. Restore them in Wafer Keep with your recovery words and choose a new PIN. Your money is on the blockchain, not on the phone, so nothing is lost while you have the words.

What if the phone is lost or broken?

Set up another phone and restore with your recovery words. Then pair it with Wafer Wallet again. Someone who finds the old phone still needs the PIN, and gets ten tries.

Which coins and networks does it sign for?

Ethereum, BNB Chain and the other Ethereum-style networks Wafer Wallet supports, with their coins and tokens such as USDT and USDC; Bitcoin (native SegWit, bc1q…); and Solana with SOL, USDC and USDT. dApps and swaps in Wafer Wallet are signed on Keep too.

Does Wafer Shield work with Wafer Keep?

Yes, as a dApp inside Wafer Wallet. Moving funds into Shield is signed on Wafer Keep. Your private balance is opened with one signature from Keep and then works on the online device while Shield is open, as with any wallet.

Can I use it with MetaMask or other wallets?

No. Wafer Keep talks only to Wafer Wallet, through its own encrypted codes. That is what lets each side check the other.
